Privacy Policy for Unhook

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Unhook helps you spend less time on social media. The app is built so that your data stays on your own phone. This page explains what the app does and does not do.

In short

  • Unhook does not collect any personal information.
  • Nothing is sent to the developer, to a server or to third parties.
  • The app has no ads, no tracking and no analytics tools.
  • The Android app does not have permission to use the internet.

What the app stores on your phone

The following is stored locally on your device, and only there:

  • Your settings: your “why” text, alternatives to scrolling, which apps you have selected, blocking times, limits and the code lock.
  • Sessions: when you used the selected apps, for how long and which app.
  • Triggers you choose to log, with any notes.
  • How much time you have spent in the selected apps today, which counts towards the daily limit.
  • If you turn on the code lock, a scrambled version of your code (a salted hash), never the code itself.

You can delete everything at any time under More → Privacy → Delete all data. If you uninstall the app, all data is removed as well.

Permissions in the Android app and why they are needed

  • Usage access: Used to see which app is open right now, so Unhook can count time in the apps you have selected and notify you. The information is processed on the phone and never leaves it.
  • Display over other apps: Used only to show a lock screen when a selected app is locked.
  • Notifications: Used to send you suggestions for something else to do. You can turn them off at any time in your phone settings.
  • Foreground service: Keeps the monitoring running in the background, and is shown as the “Unhook is watching” notification.
  • Start at boot: Restarts the monitoring after the phone has been restarted.
  • Biometrics: Used only if you choose to unlock the app with your fingerprint. Unhook never receives your fingerprint data. Android handles it and only tells the app whether it matched.
  • List of installed apps: Used only so you can choose which apps should count. The list never leaves the phone.

The web version

The web version of Unhook stores its data in your browser’s local storage on your device. It includes its own fonts instead of loading them from third parties, and it sends no data out.

Sharing with others

We do not share any data with anyone, because we do not have access to it.

Children

Unhook is not aimed at children under 13 and does not collect information from anyone.
