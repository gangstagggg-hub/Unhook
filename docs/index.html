      Automatic tracking and blocking

      This version runs in the browser, and browsers are not allowed to see or stop other apps. That is why you have to start sessions yourself here.

      Automatic sessions, a notification when you open Instagram or Snapchat, and blocking at set times require the Android app.

      Apps

      Unhook tracks your time in these apps. Turn on the switch for the ones that should be locked during blocking times, have a timeout and count towards the daily limit. Tap an app to give it its own time. Without its own time, the main time under Timeout applies.
